Privacy Policy — Bob's Lumber Empire

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Bob's Lumber Empire ("the app", "we") handles information when you play the app on Android. We are the developer of the app; we do not operate any servers of our own and we do not ask you to create an account.

Game progress and data stored on your device

Your game progress (cash, capacity, upgrades) is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage inside the app sandbox. We cannot read, receive or transfer it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted. Clearing app data in Android Settings deletes your progress. The app does not offer a cloud save feature.

Information collected by third parties

The app includes two third-party SDKs from Yandex. They are the only components that transmit information off your device. We do not add any of our own analytics or advertising code.

Yandex Mobile Ads (Yandex Advertising Network)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads. They appear only when you choose to watch one, and they are the only way the developer earns revenue. No ads are shown otherwise.

To serve and measure ads, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and device model;
  • Approximate location, inferred from your IP address;
  • App interactions, such as rewarded-ad display events and errors;
  • Crash logs and diagnostics related to the advertising SDK;
  • IP address and network state used to deliver ads.

Yandex Mobile Ads may use this information for ad selection, measurement and frequency capping under its own policies. Read them here: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

Yandex AppMetrica (analytics)

The app includes Yandex AppMetrica, an analytics SDK that provides us with anonymous usage statistics. It may collect:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and device model;
  • Approximate location, inferred from IP address;
  • App interactions, such as app start, session length and rewarded-ad events;
  • Crash logs and diagnostics that help us find and fix problems;
  • IP address and network state.

This data is used in aggregate to understand how the game is used and to improve stability. It is not used to build advertising profiles of you by us, and we cannot associate it with a person. AppMetrica's terms: https://appmetrica.yandex.ru/, and https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/

Encryption in transit

All data transferred by these SDKs is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, files, contacts, calendar, messages, health or financial data, precise GPS location, web browsing history, or advertising preferences for our own purposes. We do not operate our own analytics or advertising servers and we do not run our own trackers.

Sharing and transfer

We do not sell your information. Data collected by the SDKs is processed by Yandex, which stores it on servers located in Russia and other countries. You can learn about Yandex's cross-border data handling in its privacy information linked above.

Deleting your data

You can delete the data stored on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Bob's Lumber Empire → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. This also deletes your game progress. Because the app has no server-side account, we hold no copy of your progress and cannot delete anything for you; the identifiers mentioned above are tied to the device and are removed when the app is uninstalled or the data is cleared.

Permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET — required to serve ads and to load web fonts.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used to check whether a connection is available before requesting ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — allows the advertising SDK to access the resettable Advertising ID, which Yandex Mobile Ads and AppMetrica use for advertising and analytics.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or phone permissions.

Children

This game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Advertising is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information for our own purposes. Because the app contains no accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information, it is not directed at children and we do not apply an age-based design label.

Network and offline use

The app uses the internet to load web fonts and to serve advertising. Core gameplay works offline. Advertising requires an internet connection; if you choose not to connect, ads will not load and your progress still saves locally.
